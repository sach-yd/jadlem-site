Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Jadłem is a calorie and nutrition tracker. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: Jadłem has no servers of its own, so there is no Jadłem account database — signing in is optional and uses Sign in with Apple. We don't collect your personal data, we don't sell it, and we don't track you. The one thing you can choose to share is the details of a product you add to the community catalogue, described below.

Signing in (optional)

You can use Jadłem as a guest, or sign in with Apple to keep your log under your own account, separate from anyone else who uses the same device. Signing in is handled by Apple; Jadłem never sees a password.

  • When you sign in, Apple gives Jadłem an identifier for you that is specific to this app and, the first time only, your name and email address (which may be Apple's private relay address if you choose to hide yours). Jadłem keeps these in your device's Keychain and does not send them to us or to anyone else — we have no server to send them to.
  • That identifier is stored alongside each item you log so that each account on a device sees only its own data. It syncs, with the rest of your data, through your private iCloud.
  • Each account has its own Gemini API key and its own answer to the AI photo consent question.
  • You → Account → Sign Out returns to the start screen and leaves your data in place. You → Account → Delete Account removes the account and everything in it (see below).

What stays on your device or in your iCloud

  • Your food log, drinks, weigh-ins, favourites, custom foods, Stash and settings are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud they are also synced through your private iCloud (CloudKit) database so they appear on your other devices. Only you can access that database; we cannot see it.
  • Your Gemini API key (if you add one) is kept in your device's Keychain and is not synced.
  • The AI coach runs on your device using Apple's on-device model. Your conversations and the numbers it uses stay on your device — except that when it suggests a food to log, it searches the same public food databases a manual search does (see "Services that receive data from the app" below), sending only the food name it's searching for, not your conversation.
  • On an iPhone with Apple Intelligence (iOS 27 or later), Photo mode and Label mode read your photos with Apple's on-device model. The photo is not sent anywhere and no API key is needed.
  • Voice input for the Stash and for searching by voice uses on-device speech recognition where your language supports it; otherwise it is processed by Apple's speech recognition servers, the same as Siri and other on-device dictation across iOS.
  • Reading a nutrition label with the on-device reader happens entirely on your device.

Sharing products you add (optional)

When you scan a barcode that Open Food Facts and USDA don't know and you add the product yourself, Jadłem offers to share it with all Jadłem users so that anyone who scans that barcode later finds it. The switch is on by default and you can turn it off each time you add a product.

  • What is shared: the barcode, product name, brand, serving size and the nutrition values you entered or read from the label. Nothing else — not your log, name, email, account, photos or the rest of your data.
  • Where it goes: a public database hosted by Apple (the public CloudKit database of Jadłem's iCloud container). Every Jadłem user can read it. Only people signed in to iCloud can add to it. Apple attaches an anonymous, app-specific identifier of the creating iCloud account to each entry. It doesn't include your name or email, and Jadłem's own app doesn't read or show it to anyone — but it is a standard, readable part of every record in a public database, so anyone able to query the database directly (not just through the app) could in principle see it and use it to tell that several products or reports came from the same anonymous contributor. If you'd rather not add products, leave the sharing switch off when you're asked, or don't use "Add this product yourself."
  • Only products with a barcode. Homemade foods and anything else you create stay private to you.
  • If you're offline when you add a product, it is sent the next time you open Jadłem.
  • Removal: shared products belong to the shared catalogue and contain no personal information, so they are not removed when you clear your data or delete your account. If you want a product you added taken down, or spot a wrong or inappropriate entry, use the support contact on Jadłem's App Store page.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Jadłem writes the calories, protein, carbs, fat and weight you log to Apple Health, and reads your active energy and your latest weight. Health data is used only to provide these features. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never shared with third parties and never sent to us. Importing your weight from Health copies one value into Jadłem only when you confirm it — that value is then saved as a regular weigh-in, the same as one you type in yourself, and is synced like any other weigh-in through your own private iCloud database if you're signed in to iCloud (see "What stays on your device or in your iCloud" above).

Services that receive data from the app

  • Open Food Facts, USDA FoodData Central and the community catalogue. When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the search text or barcode number is sent to these public nutrition databases (the community catalogue being the CloudKit database described above) to look up the product. No account or personal information is included. Open Food Facts' and USDA's own privacy policies apply, and Apple's applies to CloudKit.
  • Google Gemini API (optional). If Apple Intelligence isn't available on your iPhone, or if it is available but fails to read a particular photo: if you add your own Gemini API key and agree in the app, the photo you take in Photo mode (and in Label mode, if you choose AI reading), along with any follow-up answers you type, is sent to Google's Gemini API with a short instruction prompt, using your key. Jadłem does not receive or store these photos. Google handles them under its Gemini API terms and privacy policy; depending on your Google plan it may retain them or use them to improve its products. You can turn this off at any time in You → AI, or remove your key.

What we don't do

Jadłem has no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking code. It does not track you across other apps or websites.

Permissions

Camera (barcodes, food photos, labels, Stash photos), microphone and speech recognition (dictating Stash items or searching by voice), notifications (reminders you switch on) and Apple Health are each optional. You can change any of them in the iOS Settings app.

Your data and deleting it

You → Data → Clear All Data removes everything the current account (or guest) has logged in Jadłem, including the copies synced through your iCloud; other accounts on the device are not affected. You → Account → Delete Account removes the account itself as well — its data, targets and settings, saved name and email, and AI key — on this device and in your iCloud. To also stop Jadłem using your Apple ID, go to iOS Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple. Food entries Jadłem saved to Apple Health are removed too; weigh-ins already in Health stay there and are managed in the Health app. Deleting the app removes its data from your device, except the Gemini API key and the sign-in details iOS keeps in the Keychain, which iOS may not remove with the app — Delete Account, or removing the key in You → AI, clears them. You can also manage iCloud data in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud.

Children

Jadłem is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the app and on the App Store page, with a new date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support contact on Jadłem's App Store page.
