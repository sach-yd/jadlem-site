Jadłem — Support

Jadłem is a calorie and macro tracker for iPhone with Apple Watch, widgets and Siri Shortcuts. There is no Jadłem server and no ad or analytics tracking, so most questions are answered below.

Get help or report a problem

Open an issue on the Jadłem support tracker (a free GitHub account is needed) and describe what happened, your iPhone model and iOS version. Please do not post personal health data. Issues are read and answered there.

Take down or report a community product

Shared products are visible to all Jadłem users. You can report a product from its page in the app (Report this product); three independent reports hide it automatically. To ask for a product to be removed, open an issue as above and name the product and brand.

Your data

  • Clear everything you logged: You → Data → Clear All Data.
  • Delete your account: You → Account → Delete Account. To stop Jadłem using your Apple ID, also go to iOS Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple.
  • Apple Health: permissions are managed in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.
  • Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Common questions

  • Photo AI says it is unavailable. Apple Intelligence on iOS 27 is used when available. Otherwise add your own Google Gemini key in You → AI.
  • My data is not on my other device. Make sure you are signed in to the same iCloud account and, if you use Sign in with Apple, the same Apple ID on both devices.
  • A barcode is not found. Add it as a custom food; you can choose to share it with the community catalogue.
